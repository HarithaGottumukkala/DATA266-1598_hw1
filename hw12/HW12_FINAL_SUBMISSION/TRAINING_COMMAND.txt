PART 1 - GPTQ  (notebooks/DATA266_HW12_Part1_GPTQ_executed.ipynb, NVIDIA L4)

          peak = torch.cuda.max_memory_allocated(); FP16 re-saved in FP16 for the size comparison
cell 31   FP16 perplexity re-run in the final software environment
